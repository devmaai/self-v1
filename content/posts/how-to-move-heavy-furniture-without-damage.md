**Introduction**

The safest way to move heavy furniture is to empty and take it apart first, wrap it in moving blankets, then slide, roll or carry it with the right tools: furniture sliders on floors, a dolly for long distances and lifting straps for carrying. With two people and about $30 to $80 in basic gear, you can move most sofas, dressers and bookcases without scratching your floors, scuffing walls or straining your back. This guide covers the tools you need, how to prepare each piece, safe lifting, getting furniture up and down stairs, protecting floors and doorways, loading a truck or storage unit, and when it makes sense to call in help.

## What tools do you need to move heavy furniture?

You need four basic tools to move heavy furniture safely: furniture sliders, moving blankets, a dolly and lifting straps. Most can be bought or rented cheaply, and many truck rental counters lend dollies and pads for a small fee.

| Tool | What it does | Typical cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Furniture sliders | Let heavy pieces glide across floors and carpet | $10 to $25 for a set |
| Moving blankets or pads | Protect wood, corners and walls from scratches | $15 to $40 for a set, or rent with a truck |
| Hand truck or appliance dolly | Rolls tall, heavy items like dressers and fridges | $10 to $15 a day to rent, $40 to $150 to buy |
| Four-wheel furniture dolly | Carries wide, flat pieces like sofas and tables | $10 to $40 |
| Lifting straps | Spread the weight across two people's shoulders or forearms | $15 to $35 |
| Stretch wrap and tape | Holds drawers and doors shut, keeps pads in place | $10 to $25 |
| Basic tool kit | Removes legs, feet, hardware and bed frames | Usually already at home |

Always strap a tall piece to the dolly so it cannot tip.

## How to prepare heavy furniture before you move it

Preparation removes weight and weak points before anyone lifts a thing, which is where most damage is prevented.

- Empty everything. Take out drawers, shelves, books and dishes. Contents add weight and can shift and break joints.

- Take it apart. Remove table legs, bed rails, sofa feet, mirrors on dressers and glass shelves. Put screws and bolts in labeled zip bags and tape each bag to its piece.

- Secure moving parts. Tape or stretch wrap doors and drawers closed. Avoid tape directly on finished wood, since it can lift the finish.

- Wrap it. Cover the piece in moving blankets and hold them with stretch wrap. Add cardboard corners or extra padding on sharp edges.

- Measure the route. Check the height and width of each doorway, hallway and stair turn against the furniture. If it will not fit, plan to remove doors or hinges first.

Wrap glass tops, marble and mirrors separately and stand them on edge.

## How to move heavy furniture by yourself

You can move heavy furniture by yourself across a single floor using sliders, leverage and a dolly, but anything you have to lift or carry should be a two-person job.

For solo moves on one level:

- Tilt and slide. Tip one side of the piece up slightly, slip a slider under each corner, then push low and steady from the base rather than pulling from the top.

- Use a lever. A furniture lifter or a sturdy pry bar lets you raise one end just enough to place sliders.

- Dolly it. Tilt the piece onto a hand truck, strap it in and lean it back to roll.

### How to move heavy furniture on carpet

On carpet, hard plastic sliders work best because soft felt sliders grab the pile. Push in a straight line, keep the load low, and lay a sheet of hardboard or plywood on thick carpet if you are rolling a dolly across it.

### How to move a heavy dresser or couch

For a heavy dresser, remove every drawer first and carry them separately, then move the frame upright on a hand truck. For a couch, take off cushions and legs, then carry it on its end through tight doorways. Many sofas fit through a door when you tilt them vertically and hook the arm around the frame.

## How to lift heavy furniture safely

Lift with your legs, keep the load close to your body and never twist while carrying. Good technique prevents most moving injuries.

- Squat down with your feet shoulder-width apart and your back straight.

- Grip the solid frame, not legs, handles or trim, which can snap off.

- Stand up using your legs and keep the piece close to your waist.

- Turn your whole body with your feet instead of twisting at the waist.

- Agree on a single count before every lift, so both people move together.

- Wear gloves and closed-toe shoes, and take breaks.

Lifting straps take a lot of the strain off your hands and back by putting the weight on larger muscle groups. They work best with a strong partner at the low end.

## How to move furniture up stairs (and down)

To move furniture up stairs, put the strongest person at the bottom, carry the piece at an angle, and move one step at a time with clear communication. The person below supports most of the weight, while the person above guides and steadies.

- Clear the staircase and turn on every light before you start.

- Pad the banister and the wall corners at each turn.

- Use a hand truck with stair climbers or a padded dolly on straight flights, rolling one step at a time.

Going down, keep the heaviest end at the bottom and never let a piece slide freely down the steps.

## How to protect floors, walls and doorways

Protecting your home costs very little and prevents the most common moving damage.

| Surface | How to protect it |
| --- | --- |
| Hardwood, tile and laminate | Felt sliders, floor runners, rubber-backed mats or hardboard sheets |
| Carpet | Plastic carpet film and hard plastic sliders |
| Door frames | Foam or cardboard door jamb protectors |
| Walls and corners | Moving blankets or corner guards taped at hip and shoulder height |
| Banisters | Wrapped in moving blankets or foam pipe insulation |

## How to load heavy furniture into a truck or storage units

Load the heaviest pieces first, against the front wall of the truck or the back wall of the unit, then build forward in tight, even layers.

- Stand sofas and mattresses on end to save floor space.

- Place dressers and cabinets against the walls with their backs facing out.

- Fill gaps with boxes and soft items so nothing can shift in transit.

- Strap each layer to the truck's tie points before you drive.

If you are using a [moving container](/blog/how-do-moving-containers-work-costs-companies-and-tips-2026), follow the same order and use the container's tie-down rails.

### How to load self storage units safely

Self storage units are easiest to load when you plan an aisle down the middle and keep the items you will need soon near the door. Ask the facility about drive-up access, carts and freight elevators before moving day, and put pallets or plastic sheeting under wood furniture if the floor may get damp. Our [storage size guide](/storage-size-guide) and [10x10 storage unit](/blog/10x10-storage-unit-size-price-and-what-fits-inside) guide help you choose the right space.

### Protecting heavy furniture in self storage

Keep wood and leather furniture covered with breathable cotton sheets rather than plastic, which can trap moisture. For long stays or humid climates, a [climate controlled unit](/climate-controlled-storage) protects finishes, glue joints and upholstery. ## When to hire help to move heavy furniture

Hire help when an item is too heavy for two people, needs to go up narrow or turning stairs, or has high value. Pianos, gun safes, pool tables, marble tables and large appliances are common examples.

| Item or job | Typical cost to hire help (local) |
| --- | --- |
| Labor only crew for heavy items | $40 to $80 per mover per hour, 2 to 4 hour minimum |
| Moving a few pieces of furniture | About $150 to $350 in total |
| Upright piano | About $150 to $500 |
| Grand piano | About $200 to $1,500 depending on size |
| Pool table with reassembly | About $200 to $600 |
| Stairs surcharge | About $40 to $100 per flight |

A labor only crew is often the most cost-effective option because you keep control of the truck while they handle the lifting. ## Frequently asked questions

What is the easiest way to move heavy furniture? Empty the piece, take off legs and loose parts, then use furniture sliders on floors and a strapped dolly for longer distances. This lets you move most items with little or no lifting.

How do you move heavy furniture without scratching floors? Use felt or rubber sliders on hard floors, lay floor runners or hardboard along the route, and never drag furniture directly on its legs.

Can one person move heavy furniture alone? One person can slide or dolly heavy furniture across a single floor, but lifting, carrying and stairs should always involve at least two people.

How do you move a heavy dresser? Remove all drawers and carry them separately, wrap the frame in a moving blanket, then strap it upright to a hand truck and roll it.

What is the best way to move furniture up stairs? Put the strongest person at the bottom, carry the piece at an angle one step at a time, and use lifting straps or a stair-climbing hand truck for very heavy items.

How much does it cost to have movers move heavy furniture? Many local jobs cost about $150 to $350 for a few pieces, while labor crews charge around $40 to $80 per mover per hour. Pianos, safes and stairs add to the price.

How should I store heavy furniture after a move? Store it clean, dry and covered with breathable sheets, keep heavy pieces on the floor against the walls, and choose climate control for long stays or valuable wood and leather.
