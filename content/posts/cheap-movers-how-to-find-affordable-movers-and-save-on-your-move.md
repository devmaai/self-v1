**Introduction**

The cheapest way to hire movers is usually a labor only crew for a local move, which costs about $200 to $900, compared with $800 to $2,500 for a full service local move. For long distance moves, a moving container or a mix of a rental truck and hired loaders often costs far less than a full service company. This guide shows what cheap movers really cost, the cheapest ways to move, how to compare affordable moving companies, how to check a mover before you book, and how to save even more with smart timing and short-term storage.

## How much do cheap movers cost?

Cheap movers for a local move typically cost $100 to $200 an hour for a two-person crew with a truck, and most small local moves land between $400 and $1,200. Long distance moves cost more because of fuel, drive time and weight.

| **Move type** | **Typical cost** | **Best budget option** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Studio or 1 bedroom, local | $400 to $1,200 | Labor only crew plus a rental truck |
| 2 to 3 bedrooms, local | $1,000 to $2,500 | Small full service crew, booked midweek |
| 1 to 2 bedrooms, long distance | $1,500 to $5,000 | Moving container or rental truck with hired loaders |
| 3 or more bedrooms, long distance | $4,000 to $10,000 or more | Container or freight style move with labor at each end |

What decides your price:

- **Distance:** local moves are billed by the hour, while long distance moves are usually priced by weight or volume and miles.

- **Volume:** the less you move, the smaller the truck, crew and bill.

- **Date:** weekends, month end and summer are the most expensive times to move.

- **Access:** stairs, elevators and long walks to the truck add labor time.

- **Extras:** packing, crating and specialty items such as pianos raise the quote.

## What is the cheapest way to move?

The cheapest way to move is to do most of the work yourself and pay only for the parts you cannot do. That usually means one of these four options.

- **DIY with a rental truck.** You rent a truck from a company such as [U-Haul](<https://www.uhaul.com/>) or [Penske Truck Rental](<https://www.pensketruckrental.com/>), load it with friends and drive it yourself. This is the lowest cost choice for short local moves.

- **Rental truck plus hired labor.** You drive, and a labor only crew handles the heavy lifting. Our guide to [hiring loading and unloading help](<https://www.selfstorage.help/blog/moving-help-how-to-hire-loading-and-unloading-labor>) explains rates and what to ask.

- **Moving container.** A company such as [PODS](<https://www.pods.com/>) drops a container at your home, you load it, and they transport it. Learn [how moving containers work and what they cost](<https://www.selfstorage.help/blog/how-do-moving-containers-work-costs-companies-and-tips-2026>).

- **Budget full service movers.** A small local company packs, loads, drives and unloads. This costs the most of the four, yet a lean crew booked on a quiet date can still be good value.

| **Option** | **Your effort** | **Typical savings vs full service** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| DIY rental truck | High | 50% to 80% |
| Truck plus labor only crew | Medium | 40% to 60% |
| Moving container | Medium | 30% to 50% on long distance moves |
| Budget full service | Low | Varies by date and volume |

## How to find affordable movers near you

You find affordable movers by collecting several written quotes and comparing them on the same details. Use these steps.

- **Make an inventory.** List large furniture, count boxes and note stairs or elevators at both homes.

- **Choose a flexible date.** A midweek, mid-month date in fall or winter gives you the best rates.

- **Ask for three to five quotes.** Ask local movers, regional movers and labor only crews so you can compare different models.

- **Request an in-home or video survey.** A quote based on a walkthrough is far more accurate than a phone guess.

- **Compare line by line.** Look at the hourly rate or total, the minimum hours, travel time, fuel, materials and any stair or long carry fees.

- **Ask about discounts.** Many movers offer lower rates for students, seniors, military families and off-peak bookings.

### Questions to ask cheap moving companies

- Is this a binding estimate, a non-binding estimate or a not-to-exceed price?

- What fees could be added on moving day?

- Do you charge for travel time to and from my home?

- Are blankets, dollies, tape and shrink wrap included?

- How do you handle damage claims, and what valuation coverage is included?

- What deposit do you require, and is it refundable?

## How to check that a cheap mover is legitimate

A legitimate mover gives you a written estimate, has clear contact details and, for interstate moves, holds a USDOT number registered with the federal government. Always check before you pay a deposit.

- **Look up the USDOT number.** Interstate movers must be registered with the [Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration](<https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/protect-your-move>), and you can search their records for free.

- **Get the estimate in writing.** A quote that is given only over the phone leaves room for surprises.

- **Watch the deposit.** Reputable movers usually ask for a modest deposit, so a request for a large cash payment up front is a reason to pause.

- **Read recent reviews.** Focus on comments about final bills, care with furniture and punctuality.

- **Check the paperwork.** For interstate moves you should receive the federal "Your Rights and Responsibilities When You Move" booklet and a bill of lading.

The FMCSA also shares [tips for a successful move](<https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/protect-your-move/tips-for-success>) that walk you through each stage.

## When is the cheapest time to hire movers?

The cheapest time to hire movers is midweek, mid-month, between October and April. Demand peaks from May to September and at the end of every month, when leases end.

- Book Tuesday to Thursday for lower hourly rates and more crew availability.

- Avoid the last and first few days of the month.

- Ask for an early morning start so the job finishes within the minimum hours.

- Book four to six weeks ahead for summer, or one to two weeks ahead in quieter months.

## Easy ways to lower your moving quote

The fastest way to cut a moving quote is to move less and be fully ready when the crew arrives. Movers bill for time and volume, so every box you skip and every minute you save lowers the bill.

- **Declutter first.** Sell, donate or recycle items you will not use in your new home.

- **Pack yourself.** Professional packing adds hundreds of dollars. If you want help with only fragile items, see [what packing services cost](<https://www.selfstorage.help/blog/packing-services-what-they-cost-and-when-to-hire-help>).

- **Use free boxes.** Grocery stores, liquor stores and local community groups often give boxes away.

- **Disassemble furniture.** Take apart bed frames and table legs before the crew arrives.

- **Prepare the path.** Reserve close parking and clear hallways so the crew moves faster.

- **Protect large pieces.** Our guide on [moving heavy furniture without damage](<https://www.selfstorage.help/blog/how-to-move-heavy-furniture-without-damage>) helps you avoid repair costs.

## Using storage units and self storage to make your move cheaper

Short-term self storage can lower your moving bill when your move-out and move-in dates do not line up, or when you want to move on a cheaper date. Instead of paying movers to wait or making two trips, you load once into a storage unit and move the rest later.

### When self storage units save you money

- **Gap between homes:** a small unit for a few weeks often costs less than extra truck days or hotel nights.

- **Off-peak timing:** you can move out at month end and move in on a cheaper midweek date.

- **Downsizing:** you move only what fits the new home and decide on the rest later.

- **Staging a sale:** clearing rooms helps you show your home while you plan the main move.

## How moving prices differ by city

Moving prices follow local wages, traffic and parking, so the same two-bedroom move can cost noticeably more in a dense metro than in a smaller city. Large coastal cities such as New York, San Francisco and Los Angeles sit near the top of the range, while many Midwest and Mountain West cities sit lower.

To choose the right mover for your location:

- Favor movers based close to your current home, since shorter travel time lowers hourly charges.

- Ask whether parking permits, elevator reservations or long carries apply in your building.

- For storage during the move, pick a facility on the route between your old and new homes so you avoid extra driving.

## Frequently asked questions

**How much do cheap movers cost per hour?** A two-person crew with a truck usually costs $100 to $200 an hour for a local move. Labor only crews without a truck often charge $40 to $80 per mover per hour.

**What is the cheapest way to move across the country?** A moving container or a rental truck with hired loaders at each end is usually the cheapest long distance option. Both cost much less than a full service interstate mover.

**Are cheap moving companies safe to use?** Many affordable movers are reliable small businesses. Check the USDOT number for interstate moves, get a written estimate and read recent reviews before you book.

**How far in advance should I book affordable movers?** Book four to six weeks ahead for summer or month-end dates. In quieter months, one to two weeks is often enough.

**Is it cheaper to move on a weekday?** Yes. Tuesday to Thursday moves often cost less than weekend moves because demand is lower and crews have more open slots.

**Can I negotiate with movers?** Often, yes. Share competing written quotes, offer a flexible date and ask about discounts for students, seniors or military families.

**Should I tip cheap movers?** Tipping is optional. A common guideline is $4 to $5 per mover per hour, or about $20 to $50 per mover for a typical job.

**Can a storage unit make my move cheaper?** Yes. Short-term storage lets you move on cheaper dates, bridge a gap between homes and avoid paying for extra truck days.
