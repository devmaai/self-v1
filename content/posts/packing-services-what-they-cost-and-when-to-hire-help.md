**Introduction**

Professional packing services in the US usually cost $280 to $2,200, and most companies charge about $60 to $80 per packer per hour. A one-bedroom apartment typically runs $280 to $650 for a full pack, while a three-bedroom home costs around $1,000 to $2,200. This guide breaks down packing costs by home size, how companies price the work, what full and partial packing include, the extra fees to plan for, and how to decide whether hiring help is worth it for your move.

## How much do packing services cost?

Most households pay between $280 and $2,200 for professional packing, depending on how much you own and how many packers the job needs. Here is what a full pack typically costs by home size.

| Home size | Typical packing time | Typical crew | Typical cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Studio or 1 bedroom | 2 to 4 hours | 2 packers | $280 to $650 |
| 2 bedrooms | 3 to 5 hours | 2 to 3 packers | $530 to $1,000 |
| 3 bedrooms | 6 to 8 hours | 2 to 3 packers | $1,000 to $2,200 |
| 4 bedrooms | 8 to 12 hours | 3 to 4 packers | $2,000 to $3,500 |
| 5+ bedrooms | 1 to 2 days | 4+ packers | $3,000 or more |

Quick take:

- Hourly rates usually sit between $60 and $80 per packer, and higher in big coastal cities.

- An experienced packer can pack roughly 100 square feet of living space per hour.

- Supplies can add $180 to $750 if they are billed separately.

Ask for a written quote based on a walkthrough or video survey, since homes of the same size can hold very different amounts.

## How do packing companies price their services?

Most local packers bill by the hour, while many long-distance movers price packing per box or as a flat fee added to the move. Knowing which method you are being quoted makes it much easier to compare offers.

- Hourly pricing: the cost is the number of packers, times the hours, times the hourly rate. For example, 2 packers working 4 hours at $70 an hour comes to $560.

- Per-box pricing: common for long-distance moves. Expect about $10 to $25 for a standard or clothing box, around $15 for a wardrobe box, and $30 to $50 for a dish pack full of kitchenware.

- Flat-fee pricing: less common, and usually tied to a fixed inventory. A three-bedroom home might be quoted around $900 to $1,500 for packing alone.

- Materials: some companies include boxes, tape, paper, and bubble wrap in the rate, and others bill them separately. Always ask which applies.

## Full packing vs partial packing vs unpacking services

Full packing means the crew packs everything in your home, partial packing covers only the rooms or items you choose, and unpacking services set up your new home after delivery. Choosing the right level is the easiest way to control the cost.

| Service level | What is included | Best for | Typical cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Full packing | Every room, closet, and cabinet, plus supplies if included | Busy schedules, large homes, long-distance moves | $280 to $3,500+ |
| Partial packing | Selected rooms such as the kitchen, or fragile items only | Saving money while protecting breakables | Often 30% to 60% of a full pack |
| Fragile-only packing | Dishes, glassware, artwork, mirrors, electronics | Renters who pack the easy rooms themselves | A few hundred dollars for most homes |
| Unpacking | Unboxing, placing items, and removing empty boxes | Settling in fast after a big move | Similar hourly rate, usually fewer hours |
| Packing and unpacking services | Both ends of the move handled by the same team | Families, relocations, seniors downsizing | Roughly 1.5 to 2 times a full pack alone |

## What extra packing costs should you budget for?

Extra charges can add a few hundred dollars to a packing quote, so ask about each one before you book.

- Packing supplies: about $180 to $750 depending on home size, if they are not included.

- Custom crating: wooden crates for artwork, mirrors, marble tops, or antiques often cost $100 to $500 or more per item.

- Specialty items: pianos, chandeliers, large TVs, and grandfather clocks may need special materials or extra time.

- Short-notice bookings: last-minute jobs, especially in peak summer, often carry a premium.

- Travel or fuel fees: some companies charge for time or miles to reach your home.

- Stairs and access: walk-ups, long hallways, and elevator bookings can stretch the job.

Packing services do not usually include loading the truck. If you want help carrying boxes and furniture too, our upcoming guide to moving help for loading and unloading explains hourly labor rates.

## When is it worth hiring professional packers?

Professional packing services are worth it when your time is limited, your home is large, or you own many fragile or valuable items. If you have a small home, a flexible schedule, and few breakables, packing yourself usually saves the most money.

**Hiring help makes sense when:**

- You are moving with less than two weeks' notice.

- You have a three-bedroom home or larger.

- You own fine china, art, antiques, or electronics you want packed by trained hands.

- You are relocating for work and your employer covers moving costs.

- You want movers that pack for you so the shipment qualifies for the mover's full liability coverage, which can be limited on boxes you packed yourself.

**Packing yourself makes sense when:**

- You live in a studio or one-bedroom.

- You have several weeks to pack a room at a time.

- Your budget is tight and most of your items are clothes, books, and sturdy goods.

## How to pay less for packing services

You can cut the cost of packing services by reducing what needs packing, booking at the right time, and doing part of the work yourself.

- Declutter first. Every box you sell, donate, or recycle is a box you are not paying to pack.

- Pack the easy rooms yourself. Leave the kitchen and fragile items to the pros.

- Book midweek and off-season. Rates and availability are usually best from October through April.

- Get three written quotes. Compare hourly rates, minimum hours, and whether supplies are included.

- Bundle services. Movers often discount packing when you book it with the move.

## How to choose a packing service you can trust

Choose a company that gives a written estimate, explains its insurance or valuation coverage, and has steady recent reviews. These checks protect your belongings and your budget.

- Ask about training and experience with fragile and high-value items.

- Confirm coverage for items damaged during packing, and how claims work.

- Check registration for interstate moves. Movers crossing state lines should have a USDOT number you can look up with the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration.

- Get the estimate in writing, including hourly rate, minimum hours, supplies, and any extra fees.

- Be cautious about large deposits, especially for a local job.

## Using self storage units with packing services

Many packing companies can pack your home and deliver boxes straight to a storage unit, which is useful when your move-out and move-in dates do not line up. Self storage also works well when you are staging a home for sale or downsizing before a move.

### Tips for packing items for self storage

- Use uniform box sizes so stacks are stable inside the unit.

- Label each box on two sides with the room and a short list of contents.

- Choose climate controlled storage for wood furniture, electronics, photos, and documents. Our climate controlled storage page explains when it is worth it.

- Pick the right size. Most one or two-bedroom homes fit in a 10x10 storage unit, and our storage size guide helps with larger homes.

### How much do storage units cost during a move?

A storage unit for a month or two during a move usually costs $75 to $300 a month depending on size and location. Our guide to how much a storage unit costs covers prices by size, and short-term storage explains flexible month-to-month options. If you would rather load at home, moving containers can be packed by the same crew and stored until you are ready.

Packing and storage prices both follow local labor and real estate costs, so expect higher rates in places like New York City, San Francisco, and Los Angeles, and lower rates in cities like Kansas City, St. Louis, and Salt Lake City. Pick a facility close to your new home, with easy truck access and hours that fit your moving day.

## Frequently asked questions

How much do packing services cost? Most professional packing services cost $280 to $2,200. A one-bedroom home typically runs $280 to $650, and a three-bedroom home about $1,000 to $2,200.

How much do movers charge to pack per hour? Most movers charge about $60 to $80 per packer per hour. Rates are often higher in large coastal cities and during peak summer season.

How long does it take professionals to pack a house? A two or three person crew can usually pack a one-bedroom home in 2 to 4 hours and a three-bedroom home in 6 to 8 hours.

Do movers pack for you? Many full-service moving companies offer packing as an add-on. You can book a full pack, a partial pack for selected rooms, or fragile-only packing.

Are packing supplies included in the price? It depends on the company. Some include boxes, tape, and paper in the rate, while others bill supplies separately, which can add $180 to $750.

Is it cheaper to pack yourself? Yes. Packing yourself usually costs only $100 to $300 in supplies. Many people save money by packing easy rooms and hiring pros for the kitchen and fragile items.

When should I book packing services? Book two to four weeks ahead in the off-season and four to eight weeks ahead for a summer move. Most crews pack one or two days before moving day.
