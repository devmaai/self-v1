**Introduction**

A storage unit in Oakland, California costs about $60 to $480 a month depending on size, and the popular 10x10 unit typically runs $130 to $220, with recent citywide averages landing between about $150 and $195. That makes Oakland one of the pricier storage markets in the country, a little below San Francisco but well above inland cities like Stockton and Sacramento. This guide breaks down current Oakland storage prices by size, shows which neighborhoods cost the most and least, explains when climate control and vehicle storage are worth it, lists the fees that land on your bill, and shares practical ways to pay less.

## Oakland storage unit prices by size

Here is what Oakland facilities typically charge each month, based on current street rates across the city and nearby East Bay suburbs.

| Unit size | Typical monthly cost | With climate control | Roughly what it holds |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 5x5 | $60 to $110 | +$15 to $30 | A closet's worth of boxes and seasonal gear |
| 5x10 | $90 to $150 | +$20 to $40 | A studio or small one-bedroom apartment |
| 10x10 | $130 to $220 | +$30 to $60 | One to two bedrooms of furniture |
| 10x15 | $180 to $290 | +$40 to $75 | Two to three bedrooms plus appliances |
| 10x20 | $230 to $360 | +$50 to $95 | A three to four bedroom home, or a car |
| 10x30 | $320 to $480 | +$65 to $120 | A large home, a boat, or business stock |

Quick take:

- The 10x10 is the most rented size in Oakland, and most renters pay somewhere between $150 and $195 a month for one.

- A 5x5 locker averages around $85, so even small units cost noticeably more here than the national average.

- Online specials and discounted first months can bring a 10x10 under $110 at some facilities, so the posted rate is rarely the only price on offer.

## Why storage in Oakland costs more than most of California

Oakland storage prices sit near the top of the state for a few local reasons.

- Expensive, scarce land. Oakland is built out, and land near the freeways and the waterfront is costly, so facilities pass those costs on in monthly rents.

- Smaller homes and apartments. Many renters in Uptown, Temescal, and around Lake Merritt live in apartments without garages or basements, which keeps demand steady year round.

- Multi-story buildings. New facilities tend to go up rather than out, and indoor, elevator-access buildings cost more to run than single-story drive-up lots.

- Bay Area moves. People relocating between San Francisco, Oakland, Berkeley, and the suburbs often need a unit for a few weeks or months between leases.

- Small business demand. Contractors, makers, and online sellers in West Oakland and the flatlands use storage as overflow space for tools and inventory.

## Cheapest and priciest Oakland neighborhoods for storage

Where you book matters. The same 10x10 can cost $50 to $100 a month less depending on which part of Oakland you choose.

| Area | Price tendency | Why |

## Is climate control worth it in Oakland?

Climate control is less critical in Oakland than in hotter parts of California, but it is still worth it for valuable or sensitive items and for longer stays. The upgrade usually adds $15 to $60 a month.

It is worth paying for if:

- You are storing near the estuary, the waterfront, or the flatlands, where marine fog and damp winter air can lead to mildew over time.

- You are storing wood or leather furniture, electronics, artwork, documents, photos, vinyl records, or musical instruments.

- Your items will stay in storage for more than a few months.

- You are storing through late summer and early fall, when Oakland's warmest days arrive and a closed metal unit heats up quickly.

You can usually skip it for tools, bikes, camping gear, patio furniture, and short stays. Oakland's mild weather means many indoor units stay reasonably stable even without full climate control. For a full breakdown, read what climate controlled storage covers.

## Car, RV, and boat storage costs in Oakland

Vehicle storage is harder to find in Oakland than in the suburbs, and it costs more. Expect outdoor parking to start around $100 a month for a car.

| Type of vehicle storage | Typical monthly cost | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Uncovered parking space | $100 to $220 | Cars, trailers, and small boats |
| Covered parking space | $170 to $320 | RVs and boats that need sun and rain protection |
| Enclosed 10x20 unit | $230 to $360 | Classic cars and long-term car storage |
| Enclosed 10x30 or larger | $320 to $520 | Trucks, larger boats, and a car plus household goods |

Most RV and boat parking sits in East Oakland, San Leandro, and Hayward, where facilities have room for large lots. If you keep a boat at a marina in Alameda or Jack London Square, book a covered space early, since they fill before summer. ## Extra fees to budget for in Oakland

The advertised monthly rate is rarely your full bill. Plan for some combination of these.

| Fee | Typical cost | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Administration fee | $25 to $35, one time | Charged at move-in at most facilities |
| Security deposit | $0 to one month's rent | Often waived, refundable where it applies |
| Insurance or protection plan | $10 to $30 a month | Your renters or homeowners policy may already cover stored items |
| Disc lock | $10 to $20, one time | Some facilities require their own lock |
| Late fee | Set by your rental agreement | California law limits how much can be charged |
| Rent increases | Often 5% to 10% after 6 to 12 months | Ask how often rates change before you sign |

California's self storage law sets rules for late fees, lien notices, and lien sales, so read the late payment section of your rental agreement before you sign. If you plan to use your own insurance, check the off-premises limit, since many policies cover stored items at only a share of your total personal property coverage. ## When is storage cheapest in Oakland?

Storage in Oakland is usually cheapest from November through February, after the summer moving season has passed.

- May through August is the priciest stretch, when lease turnovers, home sales, and graduations overlap.

- Late May is busy near local campuses, so students at nearby UC Berkeley and other East Bay campuses should reserve a unit a few weeks before move-out.

- Fall and winter bring the best promotions, including discounted first months and waived admin fees.

If your dates are flexible, booking a few weeks before the summer rush gets you more choice of sizes and a better starting rate. Many facilities run move-in specials, and our guide to first month free storage deals explains how to spot an offer that actually saves you money.

## How to get a cheaper storage unit in Oakland

- Right-size the unit. A well-packed 10x10 beats a half-empty 10x15. Check the 10x10 size guide and the 5x10 size guide before you book.

- Look toward East Oakland and the Coliseum area. Facilities in the flatlands and near Hegenberger Road usually cost less than Downtown or Rockridge for the same size.

- Choose an upper floor or a standard unit. Elevator-access and non-climate units often cost less than ground-floor climate controlled space.

- Reserve online. Web-only rates and move-in specials usually beat the walk-in price.

- Ask about the rate after the promo. Confirm when and by how much your rent can rise.

- Skip the protection plan if you are covered. Use your renters or homeowners policy if it already covers stored belongings.

- Consider a locker for small loads. If you only have a few boxes, a storage locker can cost far less than a full unit.
