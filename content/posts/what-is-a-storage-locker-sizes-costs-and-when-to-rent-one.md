**Introduction**

A storage locker is a small, enclosed self storage space, usually 2x2 to 5x5 feet, that rents for about $20 to $60 a month in most US cities. Lockers are often stacked two high inside a climate controlled building, so you get a secure spot for boxes, documents, and seasonal gear without paying for a full room. This guide covers the common locker sizes, what they cost, what fits inside, how a locker compares with a standard storage unit, and the questions to ask before you rent one.

## What is a storage locker?

A storage locker is the smallest type of rentable self storage. It is a lockable compartment, usually with a hinged metal door rather than a roll-up door, located inside a building and reached by a hallway or elevator.

Most storage lockers share a few traits:

- Small footprint: typically between 4 and 25 square feet of floor space.

- Indoor location: almost always inside a building, so items stay out of rain, dust, and direct sun.

- Stacked layout: many lockers sit in two tiers, with an upper and lower row along the same wall.

- Hinged or small roll-up door: you secure it with your own padlock or disc lock.

- Hand-carry access: you bring items in by hand or on a cart, not by driving up to the door.

Facilities use different names for the same thing, including "mini storage," "small storage unit," "storage cube," and "locker unit." If the space is 5x5 feet or smaller, it usually falls in the locker category.

## Common storage locker sizes

Storage locker sizes vary by facility, but most fall into a handful of standard footprints. Height matters as much as floor space, since lockers range from about 2 feet to 8 feet tall.

| Locker size | Floor space | Typical height | Roughly what it holds |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2x2 or 2x3 | 4 to 6 sq ft | 2 to 4 ft | A few file boxes, important documents, small valuables |
| 3x3 | 9 sq ft | 3 to 4 ft | 6 to 10 small boxes, holiday decorations |
| 4x4 | 16 sq ft | 4 to 5 ft | 10 to 15 boxes, a suitcase, a small rolled rug |
| 4x6 or 5x4 | 20 to 24 sq ft | 4 to 8 ft | Seasonal clothing bins, sports gear, a desk chair |
| 5x5 | 25 sq ft | 8 ft | A closet's worth of boxes, a small dresser, a bike |

Quick take:

- If you only need to store paperwork or a few boxes, a 2x3 or 3x3 locker is usually enough.

- A 4x4 locker suits a student, a renter clearing out a hall closet, or a small business storing records.

- At 5x5, you are at the border between a locker and a small unit. Our 5x5 storage unit guide explains exactly what that size holds.

## How much does a storage locker cost?

A storage locker typically costs $20 to $60 a month, with most renters paying around $30 to $45. That makes it the cheapest way to rent self storage, since even a 5x5 unit tends to start near $35.

| Locker size | Typical monthly cost | In high-cost cities |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2x2 to 2x3 | $15 to $30 | $30 to $50 |
| 3x3 | $20 to $35 | $35 to $60 |
| 4x4 | $25 to $45 | $45 to $80 |
| 5x5 | $35 to $55 | $60 to $100 |

Several factors push a locker price up or down:

- Location: lockers in dense cities such as New York City or San Francisco can cost double the national range.

- Tier: upper-tier lockers are often cheaper than lower ones because they are harder to load.

- Climate control: many lockers include it by default since they are indoors, but some facilities add a fee.

- Promotions: move-in specials apply to lockers too, so check for first month free storage deals before you book.

Remember to budget for the same extras you would pay on a larger unit, such as a one-time admin fee of roughly $20 to $50, a lock, and insurance or a protection plan. For a full breakdown of fees and prices by size, see how much a storage unit costs.

## Storage locker vs storage unit: what is the difference?

The main difference is size and access. A storage locker is a small compartment you reach indoors on foot, while a storage unit is a larger room, often with a roll-up door, that may offer drive-up access.

| Feature | Storage locker | Storage unit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Typical size | 2x2 to 5x5 | 5x10 to 10x30 |
| Typical monthly cost | $20 to $60 | $60 to $320 |
| Door type | Hinged door or small roll-up | Full-height roll-up door |
| Access | Indoor hallway or elevator | Indoor or drive-up |
| Best for | Boxes, documents, small items | Furniture, appliances, vehicles |
| Climate control | Often included | Usually an add-on |

If you are storing even one large piece of furniture, a locker is usually too small. A 5x10 storage unit is the next step up and fits the contents of a studio apartment. To compare indoor and outdoor access in more detail, read our guide to drive-up vs indoor storage units.

## What can you fit in a storage locker?

A storage locker fits boxes, bins, and small belongings that you can carry by hand. Think in terms of what fits in a car trunk or back seat, not a moving truck.

**Good fits for a locker:**

- Important papers, tax records, and business files

- Holiday decorations and seasonal clothing

- Camping gear, small sports equipment, and golf clubs

- Collectibles, photo albums, and keepsakes

- Small electronics and spare computer equipment

- Overflow inventory for an online shop or sales rep

**Usually too big for a locker:**

- Beds, sofas, and dressers (except in a 5x5)

- Large appliances

- Full-size bicycles in smaller lockers

- Anything over about 4 feet long in a 3x3 or 4x4

The same rules apply as with any storage space, so skip hazardous materials, perishables, and anything alive. Our guide on what you cannot store in a storage unit lists the full set of restrictions.

## Who should rent a storage locker?

A storage locker is a smart choice when you need a little extra space for a long time, or a secure spot for a few important things.

People who often rent lockers:

- Apartment renters in big cities who have no basement, attic, or garage.

- College students storing a dorm's worth of boxes over the summer.

- Small business owners keeping files, samples, or marketing materials.

- Frequent travelers and remote workers who need a base for gear between trips.

- Anyone between moves who has already sold the furniture and only has boxes left.

If you are in a dense market, a locker can save you real money. In Los Angeles, for example, renting a 4x4 locker instead of a 5x10 unit can cut your monthly bill by more than half.

## Pros and cons of storage lockers

Storage lockers give you the lowest price in self storage, but the small size means you need to plan carefully.

**Pros:**

- The lowest monthly cost of any self storage option

- Indoor location protects items from weather

- Often climate controlled at no extra charge

- Easy to fill and organize, with less wasted space

- Good security, since lockers sit inside a locked building

**Cons:**

- Limited space, with no room for furniture

- Upper-tier lockers can be awkward to load

- Access may be limited to building hours rather than 24-hour entry

- Fewer available lockers at each facility, so they can sell out

If round-the-clock access matters to you, check the facility's hours before you sign. Our guide to 24-hour access storage units explains what to look for.

## How to choose the right storage locker

The right locker is the smallest one that fits your items once they are packed and stacked.

- Pack first, then measure. Box everything up, stack it at home, and measure the footprint and height.

- Check the height. A 4x4 that is 8 feet tall holds far more than a 4x4 that is 4 feet tall.

- Ask about tiers. Request a lower locker if you are storing heavy boxes, or an upper one if you want the lower price.

- Confirm climate control. It matters for documents, photos, and electronics.

- Look at access hours. Make sure you can reach the building when you need to.

- Compare a few facilities. Locker inventory is limited, so check nearby options on your city page, such as storage in San Diego or Salt Lake City.

## Frequently asked questions

What is the smallest storage unit size? The smallest storage spaces are lockers of about 2x2 or 2x3 feet. They hold a few file boxes or small valuables and usually cost $15 to $30 a month.

How much is a storage locker per month? Most storage lockers cost $20 to $60 a month. Prices are higher in big cities and lower for upper-tier lockers.

Is a storage locker the same as a storage unit? A storage locker is a type of storage unit, just a much smaller one. Lockers are usually 5x5 or smaller, indoors, and accessed on foot.

Are storage lockers climate controlled? Many are, because most lockers sit inside a heated and cooled building. Ask the facility to confirm, especially if you are storing paper, photos, or electronics.

Can you fit furniture in a storage locker? Usually not. A 5x5 locker can hold a small dresser or chair, but anything larger needs a 5x10 unit or bigger.

Do I need insurance for a storage locker? Most facilities require coverage for any rental, including lockers. You can often use your existing home or renter policy instead of buying the facility plan.

What is the difference between a storage locker and a mini storage unit? In most cases there is no difference. "Mini storage" is another name facilities use for small self storage spaces, including lockers.

How do I find a cheap storage locker? Compare a few nearby facilities, ask for an upper-tier locker, book online for web-only rates, and rent in fall or winter when promotions are most common.
