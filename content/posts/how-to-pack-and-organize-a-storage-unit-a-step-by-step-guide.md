**Introduction**

To pack a storage unit well, plan your layout before moving day, use uniform boxes packed to the top, load the heaviest items and furniture against the back wall first, stack lighter boxes on top and leave a center walkway so you can reach everything later. A careful packing plan can let you rent one size smaller, which often saves $30 to $60 a month. Below you will find the supplies to buy, a step-by-step loading order, layout ideas that keep your unit organized and the items you should keep out of your unit.

## How do you pack a storage unit step by step?

You pack a storage unit by preparing your items at home, loading the unit from the back to the front and finishing with the things you will need first near the door. Follow these steps in order:

- Sort and declutter. Sell, donate or recycle anything you would not pay to store.

- Measure your unit. Note the width, depth and ceiling height so you know how high you can stack.

- Sketch a simple layout. Mark where furniture, shelving and the walkway will go.

- Pack boxes at home. Fill each box completely, seal it and label it on at least two sides.

- Protect the floor. Lay down pallets or plastic sheeting before anything goes in.

- Load large items first. Place appliances, dressers and sofas against the back and side walls.

- Stack boxes heaviest to lightest. Put heavy boxes on the bottom and light ones on top.

- Leave a walkway. Keep a path at least 18 to 24 inches wide down the middle.

- Finish with everyday items. Place anything you may need soon right inside the door.

- Take a photo and lock up. A quick photo of each wall makes items easy to find later.

## What supplies do you need to pack a storage unit?

You need sturdy boxes in two or three standard sizes, strong packing tape, padding for fragile items and covers for furniture. Matching box sizes stack far more securely than a mix of odd shapes.

| --- | --- | --- |
| Small and medium moving boxes | Books, kitchenware, decor and most household items | About $1.50 to $3 each |
| Large boxes | Linens, pillows and other light, bulky items | About $2.50 to $4 each |
| Plastic bins with lids | Clothing, keepsakes and anything sensitive to moisture | About $10 to $20 each |

## How should you pack boxes for self storage?

Pack boxes for self storage so they are full, balanced and easy to identify. A half-empty box can crush under the weight of the boxes above it.

- Fill every box to the top. Use packing paper or towels to fill gaps so the lid does not sag.

- Keep the weight manageable. Aim for 30 to 40 pounds or less per box. Pack books and tools in small boxes.

- Wrap fragile items one by one. Stand plates on their edges and nest glasses in paper.

- Label clearly. Write the room, the contents and "fragile" or "this side up" where needed.

- Number your boxes. Match each number to a list on your phone, or tape a simple map of the unit inside the door.

- Choose plastic bins for delicate items. Clothing, photos and textiles stay safer in sealed bins.

## How do you load furniture into a storage unit?

You load furniture into a storage unit by cleaning it, taking apart anything that comes apart, wrapping it in breathable covers and placing it against the walls before the boxes go in.

- Disassemble when you can. Remove table legs, bed frames and shelves, and keep the hardware in a labeled bag taped to the piece.

- Stand sofas on end. This saves floor space. Wrap them first and add a cushion underneath.

- Use the space inside furniture. Fill dresser drawers and cabinets with light items such as linens.

- Avoid plastic wrap on wood and leather. It traps moisture. Use moving blankets or cotton sheets instead.

- Keep mattresses upright and covered. Store them flat against a wall in a mattress bag.

## Storage unit organization ideas that save space

The best storage unit organization ideas use height, keep a clear path and group items by how often you need them. These layouts work in most storage units:

### The U-shaped layout

Line the back and both side walls with furniture and boxes, and keep the center open. You can see and reach almost everything from the walkway.

### The front-to-back priority layout

- Back: long-term items such as seasonal decor, keepsakes and spare furniture.

- Middle: labeled boxes on shelving, heaviest at the bottom.

- Front: items you may need on short notice, such as tools, sports gear or documents.

### Vertical storage with shelving

Freestanding metal shelves let you stack boxes safely toward the ceiling without crushing the bottom layer. Leave a few inches of space between shelves and walls so air can move.

## What size storage unit do you need once it is packed well?

Most people need one size smaller than they expect when they pack a unit tightly and use the full height. A well-packed 10x10 holds the contents of a one to two bedroom home, while a loosely packed one may hold only a single bedroom.

## How do you protect items in self storage units?

You protect items in self storage units by keeping them clean, dry and off the floor, and by choosing climate control for anything sensitive to heat or humidity.

- Raise everything off the floor. Pallets or shelves protect against condensation and minor leaks.

- Control moisture. The EPA recommends keeping indoor relative humidity below 60 percent, ideally between 30 and 50 percent, to limit mold. Add moisture absorbers or silica gel packs to bins and drawers.

- Leave airflow gaps. Keep a few inches between your items and the walls.

- Consider climate control. Wood furniture, electronics, photos and instruments last longer in a temperature-controlled unit.

## What should you not put in storage units?

You should not store anything flammable, perishable, alive or illegal in storage units. Most rental agreements list these items as prohibited:

- Gasoline, propane, paint thinner, fireworks and other hazardous materials

- Food, pet food and anything that can spoil or attract pests

- Plants and animals

- Cash, jewelry and irreplaceable papers, which are safer at home or in a safe deposit box

Drain fuel from lawn mowers and small engines before you store them, and ask your facility for its full list of rules.

## How to choose a storage facility that makes packing easier

The right facility for easy packing offers drive-up access or wide hallways, carts and dollies, convenient hours and a location close to home. Before you sign, check:

- Whether you can drive right up to the unit or need to use an elevator

- Whether carts, dollies or loading docks are available on site

- Access hours and security features such as gated entry and cameras

Rates for the same size can change a lot from one neighborhood to the next, so compare a few facilities near you. You can search storage units in California, Utah, New York and Missouri.

## Frequently asked questions

What is the best way to pack a storage unit? Load furniture and heavy items against the back wall first, stack uniform boxes from heaviest to lightest and leave a center walkway. Keep the items you need most near the door.

How do you organize a storage unit so you can find things? Number and label every box, group items by category and keep a simple map or list on your phone. Shelving and a clear walkway make every box easy to reach.

Should you put boxes or furniture in a storage unit first? Put large furniture and appliances in first, along the back and side walls. Then fill the gaps and stack boxes in front and on top.

How high can you stack boxes in a storage unit? You can stack sturdy, full boxes up to about 6 or 7 feet, as long as the heaviest boxes sit at the bottom. Use shelving for anything higher so the stacks stay stable.

Can you store a mattress upright in a storage unit? Yes. Cover it in a mattress bag and stand it flat against a wall so it keeps its shape. Avoid bending it or placing heavy items on it.

Do I need pallets in my storage unit? Pallets are a good idea, especially in drive-up units, because they keep items off the concrete floor and protect against moisture and minor leaks.

How long does it take to pack a storage unit? Loading a well-prepared 10x10 unit usually takes two to four hours with two people. Packing boxes at home ahead of time saves the most time.
