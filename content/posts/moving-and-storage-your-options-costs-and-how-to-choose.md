**Introduction**

Moving and storage together usually adds $75 to $350 a month to your move, depending on whether you use a self storage unit, your moving company's warehouse, or a portable container. A standard 10x10 storage unit runs about $100 to $200 a month, moving company storage is often billed by weight or volume, and container storage typically costs $150 to $350 a month per container. This guide explains how each option works, what it costs, when it makes sense for a local or long distance move, and how to pick the right setup for your dates and budget.

## What does moving and storage mean?

Moving and storage means holding some or all of your belongings somewhere safe between leaving your old home and settling into your new one. You might need it for a few days, a few months, or longer, and the right option depends on how much control you want over your things.

You will usually see moving and storage used in three situations:

- **Your dates do not line up,** such as a home sale closing before your new place is ready.

- **You are moving long distance** and need time to find a permanent home after you arrive.

- **You are downsizing, renovating, or staging** a home and need extra space for furniture and boxes.

For a full picture of moving costs and timelines, start with our guide to [moving home in the US](<https://www.selfstorage.help/blog/moving-home-in-the-us-costs-timing-and-how-to-plan-your-move>).

## What are your moving and storage options?

You have three main options: rent a self storage unit and move items in yourself or with movers, let your moving company hold your shipment in its warehouse, or use a portable container that is stored at the provider's facility.

| **Option** | **How it works** | **Typical cost** | **Best for** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Self storage unit | You rent a unit by the month and load it yourself or with hired help | $100 to $200 a month for a 10x10 | Flexible timing, frequent access, local moves |
| Moving company storage | The mover keeps your shipment in its warehouse until delivery | Often about $0.40 or more per cubic foot for 30 days, or a quoted monthly rate | Long distance moves with full-service movers |
| Portable container storage | A container is dropped at your home, then stored at the provider's facility | $150 to $350 a month per container | Loading on your own schedule, then shipping or storing |

Each option trades cost against convenience and access. The sections below explain where each one fits best.

## How do self storage units work during a move?

Self storage units let you rent a space by the month, keep the only key, and visit whenever the facility is open. This is the most flexible choice when you want to reach your things during the move or when your plans could change.

Typical monthly costs for storage units during a move:

| **Unit size** | **Typical monthly cost** | **Roughly what it holds** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5x10 | $60 to $100 | A studio or small one-bedroom |
| 10x10 | $100 to $200 | A one or two-bedroom home |
| 10x15 | $150 to $220 | A two or three-bedroom home |
| 10x20 | $195 to $300 | A three or four-bedroom home |

Not sure which size to book? Our [storage size guide](<https://www.selfstorage.help/storage-size-guide>) matches your rooms to the right unit, and our guide to [how much a storage unit costs](<https://www.selfstorage.help/blog/how-much-is-a-storage-unit>) breaks down prices and fees.

**Why renters like self storage during a move:**

- Month-to-month leases, so you only pay for the time you need.

- Drive-up units make unloading a truck quick and easy.

- Large brands such as [Public Storage](<https://www.publicstorage.com/>), [Extra Space Storage](<https://www.extraspace.com/>), and [CubeSmart](<https://www.cubesmart.com/>) offer online booking and move-in promotions at many locations.

- Some truck rental companies, including [U-Haul](<https://www.uhaul.com/>), also run storage facilities, which can make it easy to book a truck and a unit together.

For stays of a few weeks to a few months, our guide to [short-term storage](<https://www.selfstorage.help/blog/short-term-storage-options-costs-and-how-it-works>) explains flexible lease options.

## How does moving company storage work?

Moving company storage, often called storage-in-transit, means your movers load your belongings, keep them in their warehouse, and deliver them when your new home is ready. You do not handle your items again until delivery day, which keeps things simple on a long distance move.

**What to know before you book:**

- **It is usually billed by weight or volume,** plus handling fees to move items into and out of the warehouse.

- **You typically cannot visit your items** while they are in storage, so pack an essentials kit to keep with you.

- **Storage-in-transit has a time limit** set by the mover's tariff or your agreement. Under federal rules, interstate movers must notify you in writing before that period ends and tell you the date it converts to permanent storage, as the [Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration](<https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/protect-your-move/how-to/subpartF>) explains.

- **Liability changes at conversion.** Once storage-in-transit becomes permanent storage, the mover's liability ends and the warehouse's rules and charges apply, so review your coverage before that date.

Ask every mover for a written quote that lists storage charges, handling fees, and the delivery fee as separate lines. The FMCSA handbook [Your Rights and Responsibilities When You Move](<https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/sites/fmcsa.dot.gov/files/2023-10/FMCSA_R%26R_Handbook_Web_v1.pdf>) is a useful checklist for interstate moves.

## How does portable container storage work?

Portable container storage means a company drops a container at your home, you load it at your own pace, and the company stores it at its facility or ships it to your new address. It suits people who want to pack on their own schedule without renting a truck.

- **Container storage often costs $150 to $350 a month** per container, depending on size and location.

- **Local container moves** commonly run about $300 to $1,100 including the first month.

- **Long distance container moves** often cost $2,000 to $7,500 or more, depending on miles and container size.

Providers such as [PODS](<https://www.pods.com/>) and [U-Pack](<https://www.upack.com/>) offer container moves with storage at their facilities, and some include a set number of storage days in the quote. Our guide to [how moving containers work](<https://www.selfstorage.help/blog/how-do-moving-containers-work-costs-companies-and-tips-2026>) covers sizes, costs, and how to compare companies.

## Which moving and storage option is best for a long distance move?

For a long distance move, moving company storage is usually the easiest choice if you are hiring full-service movers, while a container is often better value if you are happy to load it yourself. A self storage unit works best when you want to reach your things before your final delivery.

| **Your situation** | **Best fit** |
| --- | --- |
| Full-service movers, new home ready in 2 to 6 weeks | Moving company storage-in-transit |
| You want to load on your own schedule and ship once | Portable container with storage |
| Moving to a new city before buying or renting long term | Self storage unit near your new area |
| You need to reach seasonal items or work gear during the move | Self storage unit |
| Staging and selling your current home first | Self storage unit near your current home |

If you are hiring help, our guides to [packing services](<https://www.selfstorage.help/blog/packing-services-what-they-cost-and-when-to-hire-help>) and [loading and unloading labor](<https://www.selfstorage.help/blog/moving-help-how-to-hire-loading-and-unloading-labor>) show what those services cost.

## How do moving and storage costs differ by city?

Moving and storage costs follow local rents and labor rates, so a 10x10 unit or a mover's hourly rate can cost much more in a dense coastal city than in a smaller inland one. Storage in places like [New York City](<https://www.selfstorage.help/storage-search/new-york/new-york-city>) and [Los Angeles](<https://www.selfstorage.help/storage-search/california/los-angeles>) often sits well above the national average, while cities such as [Kansas City](<https://www.selfstorage.help/storage-search/missouri/kansas-city>) and [Salt Lake City](<https://www.selfstorage.help/storage-search/utah/salt-lake-city>) are usually more affordable.

**How to choose a facility based on location and convenience:**

- **Store near your new home** if you will unload within a few weeks.

- **Store near your old home** if you are staging a sale or still packing.

- **Check truck access,** with wide driveways and drive-up units for faster unloading.

- **Match access hours** to your moving day, since early starts and late finishes are common.

- **Compare three or four facilities,** because prices for the same size can vary by 30% or more within a few miles.

## How do you prepare belongings for moving and storage?

Pack for storage the same way you pack for the move, then add protection for the time your items will sit. Good preparation keeps furniture, electronics, and papers in the same condition you left them.

- **Use sturdy boxes of similar sizes** so they stack safely.

- **Label every box** on two sides with the contents and the room.

- **Keep a simple inventory** with photos, especially for moving company storage where you cannot check items.

- **Cover furniture** with breathable pads or sheets instead of plastic to avoid trapped moisture.

- **Choose **[**climate controlled storage**](<https://www.selfstorage.help/climate-controlled-storage>) for wood furniture, electronics, and documents, especially in hot or humid areas or for stays longer than a couple of months.

- **Check your insurance** before you sign, since home or renters policies may cover stored items.

## Frequently asked questions

**How much does moving and storage cost?** Storage during a move usually adds $75 to $350 a month. A 10x10 self storage unit is often $100 to $200 a month, and container storage is often $150 to $350 a month per container.

**Is it cheaper to use a storage unit or moving company storage?** A self storage unit is often cheaper for longer stays and gives you access to your items. Moving company storage can be simpler for a long distance move because the movers handle loading, storage, and delivery.

**What is storage-in-transit?** Storage-in-transit is temporary storage your mover provides while your shipment waits for delivery. For interstate moves, the mover must notify you in writing before it converts to permanent storage.

**Can I access my things in moving company storage?** Usually not. Most movers keep shipments sealed in their warehouse until delivery, so keep essentials with you.

**How long can I keep items in storage during a move?** Self storage units are usually month to month with no set end date. Moving company storage-in-transit has a time limit set by the mover's tariff or agreement.

**What size storage unit do I need for a move?** A one or two-bedroom home usually fits in a 10x10, a two or three-bedroom home in a 10x15, and a three or four-bedroom home in a 10x20.

**Do I need climate controlled storage when moving?** Choose climate control for wood furniture, electronics, artwork, and documents, or if you are storing for more than a couple of months in a hot or humid area.

**Should I store near my old home or my new home?** Store near your new home if you will unload soon, and near your old home if you are staging a sale or packing in stages.
